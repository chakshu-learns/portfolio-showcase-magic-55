# Interactive creator portfolio

## Goal
Turn Parth Sharma’s PDF portfolio into a polished, single-page website that feels personal, credible, and easy to explore on desktop and mobile.

## What I’ll build
- A bold opening section introducing Parth and his creator/content focus.
- Interactive navigation through Profile, Experience, Services, Spec Work, and Contact.
- An experience timeline with clear roles, durations, and responsibilities.
- A structured “How I can support a creator” section with six practical capabilities.
- A highlighted spec-work concept that clearly remains labeled as demonstration work.
- Direct LinkedIn and email actions, plus a downloadable copy of the original portfolio.
- Subtle scroll and interaction effects with accessible reduced-motion behavior.

## Visual direction
Editorial creator portfolio: warm off-white canvas, near-black typography, vivid coral and acid-lime accents, strong oversized headings, crisp rules, compact labels, and purposeful motion. The design will feel energetic and contemporary without weakening professional credibility.

## Technical details
- Keep the website as the main `/` page.
- Build with the existing React and Tailwind setup, using semantic theme tokens.
- Store the supplied PDF through the project’s media delivery flow for the download action.
- Add page-specific title, description, and social metadata.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, links, and PDF download in the live preview.
